Every false unreachable-function flag in this group reaches member lookup with a correctly resolved receiver and then misses — because the surface lookup searches was built without the member it needs. The fix completes the surface where it is built, not at 30 call sites.

The change in functionality

One root cause, two faces: the flat member index omits the constructor, and namespace-export lookup re-implements export resolution and never follows re-export chains. Both are fixed at the builder; the existing consumer code resolves these cases unchanged.

Rank this group against the other six

benefit vs cost

High benefit, low blast-radius. 24 durable false-flag removals across 5 projects for ~1 net line of new code plus a drift deletion — the two big arms collapse rather than expand. The only real surface is threading three already-available inputs onto the call context (mechanism B) and the regression tests. No data-model or wire-contract change; no schema bump.

fund

The constructor arm (effort 3, 14 rows) and namespace arm (effort 5, 10 rows) are both core fixes that delete drift — fund together; the operator-alias capture (effort 2, 2 rows) rides along in definition.ts.

defer / drop

The interim classifier is retired — do not author it. The sqlx eq_impl row is a maybe: triage it against the now-completed index in step 6, and only open a separate Rust receiver-resolution sub-task if it still misses.

watch — open questions

Verify Rust/JS constructor naming can't collide with an ordinary method name before keying; confirm no test asserted the old wrong-miss behavior for explicit super().__init__() once the constructor is keyed.
