The scope boundary: conditional aliases are a deliberate non-goal

The plan asked for both the direct and the if not TYPE_CHECKING: conditional alias forms; only the direct form shipped. A review reproduced that a line-range heuristic mis-keys nested-function variables — a justified boundary, not a gap.
