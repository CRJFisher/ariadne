## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->

## Functionality at stake

Pointed at microsoft/vscode, Ariadne did not produce an answer. The baseline run spent 11.23 hours and then died — `Ineffective mark-compacts near heap limit` — at file 6,634 of 8,494 discovered. The 78% it managed to index described a corpus with 603 files (7.10%) silently discarded by `ExportRegistry`, 36.5% of internal import edges pointing at one of those discarded files, and an entry-point list that changed when you renamed a directory. That is fixed and measured. On the tip (ariadne@665a1d09) a cold load of vscode's `src/` tree indexes 8,494 of 8,494 files with an empty `dropped_files` set and zero calls to `Project.remove_file`, in 421.0 s of CPU at worker width one — mean of five independent processes, CV 1.88%, reps 415.87 / 434.89 / 418.73 / 416.02 / 419.48 s — on a 6-core 32 GiB Darwin 24.6.0 x64 box under node v22.22.1. Pass A indexes across worker threads; the same corpus at width five spends 485.2 s of CPU and 219.9 s of wall on this box, where the wall is contended and so is recorded rather than budgeted.

## Is it under ten minutes: yes for one corpus, and nothing is claimed for the other

The answer is stated per corpus, because the headline is a lie without one. Over vscode's `src/` tree — the 8,494-file corpus every figure in this epic refers to — it is 7.02 minutes of CPU. The repository root is a different corpus: `find_source_files` returns 12,654 files there, and it was last measured on an earlier tree at 1,105.7 s of CPU (`RECORDED_FULL_CORPUS_BASELINE`), so no repository-root claim is made at the tip. Any claim of "under ten minutes for vscode" must say `vscode/src`.

## The two rules this investigation cost the most to learn

First, no power-law extrapolation. Every fit made here was optimistic in the same direction: a fit over n<=1,847 predicted 355 s against 778 s measured (2.19x), and the same method applied to the unpatched build predicts 1.4 h against the 11.23 h observed (16.8x). Fits cannot see the GC wall. Second, and newer: absolute CPU is machine-bound and does not transfer between sessions. The stack-only arm produced byte-identical structural output in three separate sessions — 7,891 indexed, 603 dropped, 183,018 nodes, 1,502,343 call references, 26,610 indirect entries — and measured 777.6 s, 801.3 s and 1,019.4 s. A speedup taken by dividing into another session's number was wrong by 40%: the export-gate repair's 2.202x became 1.570x when an independent verifier built their own interleaved control arm. Every ratio in this epic must come from a same-session control.

## What the user gets, and what is still open

A user can report entry points for vscode's `src/`: 8,494 of 8,494 files, nodes 202,719, resolved call edges 1,550,739, raw entry points 11,757, and the same seven-number fingerprint — plus one diagnostics `canonical_hash`, `76462ec1aa241b99` — whether files arrive in path order, reverse path order, largest-first or a seeded shuffle. The run requires an explicit `--max-old-space-size` of at least 6,144 MB: at 4,144 MB both of two processes die with `Reached heap limit`, and at 6,144 MB both complete at 5,704.3 MB peak RSS (mean of two) over a 3,905.4 MB live heap. Ariadne must not set that flag itself; TASK-393 is open against the live heap. A load killed partway through resumes from the blobs it finished. Order-independence needed one more fix on the tip than the epic's own tasks supplied: structural subtypes inferred while calls resolve left earlier callers on a narrower closure, and the four orders disagreed until commit 665a1d09 re-answered them. Still open: the pooled wall has not been taken on an idle box at this tip, because the box never went idle (its load average read 2.0 to 3.4 whenever none of this session's arms was running); and the 97.99 s computed target of TASK-381.17 was met on an earlier tree and is not re-verified here.

### Rules for every step

- One step at a time, in a worktree branched from the integration branch, merged back when its acceptance criteria pass. Two concurrent full-corpus runs on this 4-core box corrupt each other's timings.
- The task doc is the spec; the patch is a reference. Four prototype claims were refuted by verification and the docs carry the corrected figures (1.570x, not 2.202x; 5.4-6.5 GB peak RSS, not <= 5 GB; "`dropped_files` is empty" is a property of `src/`; the three residual entry points are a classifier effect). Where a patch and its doc disagree, the doc wins.
- The patches are composed, not per-task: `wf_654630da-88d-16.patch` carries TASK-381.3 and TASK-381.4, `wf_654630da-88d-19.patch` carries TASK-381.10 and TASK-381.17, and `phase2/eviction-reverse-index.patch` carries TASK-381.2 among others. Take only the hunks each step names; a patch applying cleanly is not evidence of the right subset.
- Every ratio comes from an interleaved control arm in the same session; no budget is judged against a number quoted in a doc, and no corpus-scale figure comes from a slice. Serial steps are judged on CPU; step 17 on wall taken on an idle box.
- Corpus: microsoft/vscode at f3fa55c3, `~/.ariadne/triage-entrypoints/repos/microsoft--vscode`, `src/` only — 8,494 files under Ariadne's walk. Full-corpus arms run with `--max-old-space-size=12288` until step 9 lands (peak RSS 7.83 GB on the stack alone) and `6144` after it. The flag goes on the harness command line, never inside Ariadne.
- Steps 3, 9 and 10 move the fingerprint by design: each re-baselines the harness's committed expected value and accounts for every moved member. Every other step asserts a byte-identical fingerprint against the tree immediately before it.
- A step's session is one shot: nothing re-invokes it. `ScheduleWakeup` and `Monitor` are denied, and no work is backgrounded; whatever must be waited for runs in the foreground, and the session ends only by reporting its result.
- A session spends its bound on the criteria, not on repeating the gate. The step's assertion runs `tsc --noEmit` and the whole `packages/core` suite after the session ends, so one full-suite run before the report is enough; a session whose criteria are ticked and whose commits are in the tree reports rather than re-measuring, and no benchmark arm or suite run is started that cannot finish with fifteen minutes of the bound still left for the report.
