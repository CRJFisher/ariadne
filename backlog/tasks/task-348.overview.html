Phase-1 binds every in-scope and intra-module name

The lexical scope-walk in resolve_scope_recursive is widened so every in-scope name lands as a key in the per-scope map. 104 false-positive dead-code reports across 10 corpora share one cause — removed with zero Phase-2 changes.

The one structural cause, as a path

Every one of the 104 failures runs the same path: the lexical walk can reach a name, but it never becomes a map key, so the O(1) query returns null and the definition is mislabelled unreachable.

Scope of the fault — 104 of 220

Only 104 of the 220 candidate false-positives are this binding gap. The other 116 are different faults that must not resolve here — the central over-binding risk lives in that boundary.

The precedence contract

One sentence governs every binding layer below, written as a doc-comment above the rewritten function: a name resolves iff its anchor segment is in scope, and module-visible names are always in scope of their descendants.

Before → after: resolve_scope_recursive

Sequencing & the central risk

WI-0 lands first as a pure restructure that locks the baseline; every later item is independently shippable and independently tested. WI-4 is measured before WI-5 is added; WI-6 depends on WI-4/WI-5 being in place to light up Mode B.

Central risk — over-binding into false call edges

Only 104 of 220 candidate false-positives are this fault. The other 116 (re-export, receiver-type inference, polymorphic dispatch, non-static) must not resolve here.

  • L1 is gated to module-visible defs only.
  • WI-3 fires only after export-chain and submodule both miss, for same-project resolved files.
  • L3 fires only when the anchor resolves in-scope, and binds only what member_index maps.
